A complete route through the game, for players and for testing the engine. The steps are the
walkthrough of [`../docs/flow.md`](../docs/flow.md#walkthrough) in words: 582 actions that
`engines/gilbert/tools/simulate.py` replays against the game's database to the ending, and
that a scripted run of the engine has played through its user interface, by clicks and drags,
to the outro. Nothing here is new knowledge.

## The story

Gilbert, a young dragon, crash-lands on the island in his egg, which breaks into six pieces.
To fly home he must find all six and put them back on the egg stand in the laboratory. The
islanders use the pieces as a satellite dish, a coffee cup, a window and more, and each is
won back with a little chemistry. The ruling pair of the island, the lava man under the mine
and the ice queen in her igloo on the glacier, are in love and cannot meet; settling their
quarrel opens half of the island.

| Egg | Where it is | How it comes back | Part |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | at the crash site | taken at once; it carries water first | 1 |
| 3 | the bedouin's coffee cup | the lab report and a gingerbread for the bedouin | 1 |
| 5 | in a tree in the pine forest | brown soap on the boggy ground lifts it on bubbles | 2 |
| 4 | the miner's satellite dish | a wok on the mine roof replaces it | 5 |
| 2 | the ice queen's window | a rock crystal replaces it | 6 |
| 6 | the laboratory tower | the boxes, the hazard symbols, the chair and the lever | 6 |

When the sixth piece is on the stand the outro plays and the game returns to the main menu.

## How to play

- **Walking.** Click where Gilbert should go; he walks there (hold Ctrl to run). Moving
  the mouse to the edge of the picture scrolls the room. The room has no visible hotspots: walking
  into certain places (*areas*) opens a close-up or leads to the next room. The steps say
  where each area lies (e.g. "left edge of the room"); press **H** to show them all
  (ScummVM's hotspot overlay, tinted and outlined).
- **Close-ups.** A close-up shows objects. Hover for a description, click to act, drag a
  pickable object to the inventory box (bottom right) to take it, drag an inventory item
  onto an object to use it. The arrows beside the inventory scroll it. The back arrow
  (bottom right of the picture) leaves the close-up.
- **The map.** The "Kort" button (bottom left, in rooms) opens the island map; its symbols
  ("Til stranden", "Til laboratoriet", …) take Gilbert there at once.
- **Dialogues.** Click a person to talk; pick a line. "Fortsæt" goes on, "Annuller" closes.
  A conversation's effect usually comes with its last "Fortsæt": cancelling before it
  repeats the conversation the next time.
- **Waiting.** Some things happen by themselves after a few seconds (balloons filling, the
  elevator starting, the glass cutter): stay in the close-up until they finish.
- **The book** (the scroll button) collects topics; it is not needed to finish.
- **Optional enhancements** (launcher → Game Options → Engine): always run, keyboard
  shortcuts (M map, B book, Esc back, 1–9 dialogue choices, Page Up/Down inventory), mouse
  wheel for the inventory, marked dialogue choices, new book topics highlighted, autosave on
  every room change, full-screen films, smooth or high-resolution text.

### Two dead ends (repaired)

The original has two ways to make the game unwinnable; the engine repairs both, so neither
can happen here ([flow.md "Dead ends"](../docs/flow.md#dead-ends)): leaving the igloo with
the ice queen melted but not yet in the bucket, and asking the glacier ice-cream seller for
the corkscrew before taking the bottle from the crevice. Playing the original, avoid both.

## For scripted runs (AI and testing)

Each step ends with its walkthrough action (`room R area N`, `cua C take CODE`, `cua C use
A on B`, `cua C click CODE`, `dialog D choice K`, `map E`, `back`, `wait MS`; the grammar is
flow.md's). From `engines/gilbert/tools`:

- the rules alone: `python simulate.py` (to the ending), `python simulate.py --upto N`
  (stop after step N and list what can be done);
- the engine through ge.dll's calls: `inp=$(python walkthrough_input.py 300) && sh
  run_test.sh "$inp" 1800 "" "dev_skip_films=true"`;
- the engine through its user interface (walks, clicks, drags, the map): add `--ui`;
  `--upto N` stops after step N (then `where`, `objects` and `snap` show the state).

A run ends well when the log shows `dev: film outro.mpg` and `where: mode 0 … var198 1 var199
6` with no warnings.

## Research: how players beat it

The publishers' own solution survives: Levande Böcker (the Swedish re-release) offered a
105-step solution file on its support pages, which a player recovered from the Wayback
Machine and posted in full in a Familjeliv forum thread (Swedish). Player threads on
Flashback (Swedish) and SuperDebat (Danish) give partial hints; the Swedish Wikipedia notes
the edition differences.

- [Familjeliv: "Gilbert och den kemystiska ön"](https://www.familjeliv.se/forum/thread/57525346-gilbert-och-den-kemystiska-on)
  (the official solution, §1–105)
- [Flashback: "Gilbert och den Kemystiska Ön"](https://www.flashback.org/t1491250) (hints)
- [SuperDebat: "Hjælp til Gilbert og den kemystiske ø"](https://www.superdebat.dk/debat/hj%C3%A6lp-til-gilbert-og-den-kemystiske-%C3%B8) (hints)

### Edition notes

- The official solution is for the Swedish edition; item and place names here are the
  Danish edition's own tooltips (this is the edition the engine plays).
- Wikipedia: the Norwegian Snille edition starts with lipstick, green soap and rust-proof
  paint already in the inventory (so the matchbox, towel, cooking oil and lye have no use
  there); and the element sorter does not react to the crucible or the helium balloon. In
  this edition's data the big crucible has a sorter entry (event 8312) and the helium
  balloon has none.
