## Flow

1. Arrival (entry 0): only the Fire in the bag; videos 1507, 1508, the message (72001..72010
   with videos 1499..1502), then the Mime's dialogue (70056..70066, faces 70300..70306); the
   player stands at the Mime (70300). Alberich's remarks start (timer 0, every 10 s) in his
   hall while the fire burns.
2. The console (70100, from 70001): cover 2 opens the hologram (70105 → puzzle 70102): the
   cross to 12 makes the ride 70101 → 70600 safe (else game over 1). The dam opened there
   (70108 dragged down) puts the fire and the Nib out (byte 70012).
3. 70600: the Fire used on the fire place leads to the creatures' room 70500: the Centaur,
   Dragon and Phoenix from the zooms, the Chrysoberyl from the room.
4. The Mime takes the Chrysoberyl and gives the Cage.
5. The Nib (70200): its words, then the Fire on it (if the dam has not already put it out).
6. The heater (70400 → 70410 → 70411): opening the casing and taking Loge's Tear stops the
   heater (byte 70001 = 0).
7. With the dam open, the tear out and the Cage and three creatures in the bag, the next
   arrival in the hall (`unk_19` 7) or the console handle starts Alberich's test (0x433ee0):
   three rounds on his close-up, a creature each: the Centaur, then the Dragon, then the
   Phoenix (any other order ends in game over 2).
8. After the last round (70001..70003, videos 1504, 1505) N2 is done (score 50) and the game
   goes on to RO (`GoZone(5, 0)`).
