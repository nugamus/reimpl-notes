## [Garden](../docs/jardin.md) (scene 11, painting `m04_01`)

1. Take the rake `rato` and `fuzz` (or its wings `aild`/`ailg`).
2. Click the kite `cerf`: it flies off and lands at the church (needed there).
3. Click the sheet music `partoche`: **zone 19**, place `fuzz` and the rake
   ([a04.md](../docs/a04.md)).
4. Go through the doors `porte01`/`porte02` to the inn.

## [Inn](../docs/auberge.md) (scene 1)

1. Open the bag (`saccoche`/`sac`), take the stethoscope `stetoscop`.
2. Click the cap `casquette`: **zone 20**, place the stethoscope ([a14.md](../docs/a14.md)).
3. Back to the garden (doors), walk off its edge to the wheat field.

## [Wheat field](../docs/champ.md) (scene 12)

1. Take the gun `gun` (the crows fly).
2. Click the poppies `coclicot`: **zone 22**, place the gun ([a04.md](../docs/a04.md)).
3. Walk off the field's edge to the church.

## [Church](../docs/eglise.md) (scene 13)

1. Take the sheaf `gerbe` and the kite `cerf`.
2. Click the church `eglise10`: **zone 23**, place the kite ([a04.md](../docs/a04.md)).
3. Walk back to the wheat field.

## [Wheat field](../docs/champ.md) again

1. Click the clogs `sabots`: **zone 24**, place the sheaf ([a14.md](../docs/a14.md)).
2. Walk back to the garden and into the inn.

## The ending ([inn](../docs/auberge.md))

1. With every other zone done, click the painting `tableau`: **zone 21**, the ending, the
   `cinefin` movie and the credits ([a14.md](../docs/a14.md)).
