Items cross scenes (the café needs the bridge's apple and the bedroom's parasol, the
hospital the café's teapot), so the café is visited twice.

## [Night Café](../docs/cafe.md) (scene 5, painting `m03_01`)

1. Take the lighter `briket`, the cards `carte`, the jug `pichet`, the teapot `theiere`.
2. Click the cue `ke`; when the shot ends take the shadow (`ombre`).
3. Stay in the café about 24 s: the clock turns three times. Then take it (`orloge`).
4. Click the mirror `mirroir` from mid-distance (not too close): **zone 6**, place the
   jug ([a13.md](../docs/a13.md)).
5. Optional: carry the key `clef` onto the bar door `barporte`.
6. Leave through `porte01`/`porte02` to the terrace.

## [Café terrace](../docs/terrasse.md) (scene 10)

1. Turn the crank `MANIVELLE` (the awning rolls).
2. Take the letter `lettre`, the glasses `lunette`, the die on the plate (`platode`).
3. Click `drapo`: **zone 16**, place the die ([a03.md](../docs/a03.md)).
4. Click the cap `KASKET`: **zone 17**, place the letter ([a13.md](../docs/a13.md)).
5. Walk off the terrace's far edge to the Yellow House.

## [Yellow House](../docs/maisonj.md) (scene 7)

1. Click the first pot `pot01` (rain movie); take the snail `escargot`.
2. Click the second pot `pot02` (the sun comes out; the bedroom is now the painting's).
3. Click the clock `pendule`: **zone 12**, place the café's clock ([a03.md](../docs/a03.md)).
4. Walk into the bedroom (its edge of the house).

## [Bedroom](../docs/chambreb.md) (scene 6)

1. Take the frame `cadre`, `Object02`, `Object07`, `Object09`.
2. Click the drawer `TIROIR`: **zone 9**, place `Object09` ([a13.md](../docs/a13.md)).
3. Click the chair `CHAISE1`, then the cupboard door `porte`; take the cameo `camee` and
   the parasol `ombrelle`.
4. Click the pillow `oreiller`: **zone 7**, place the frame, `Object02` and the snail
   ([a03.md](../docs/a03.md)).
5. Click the painting `tabpay`: **zone 8**, place the cameo; come back out, take the shoes,
   go in again and place them ([a13.md](../docs/a13.md)).
6. Click the painting `tab01`: **zone 11**, place `Object07`; back in the room the mirror
   breaks: take the piece `mirroirmor`.
7. Click the painting `tab02`: **zone 10**, place the shadow and the glasses.
8. Back to the Yellow House (`porte02`), then walk off its far edge to the bridge.

## [Langlois bridge](../docs/pont.md) (scene 9)

1. Walk near the apple tree: apples fall. Take the apple `apple01` and the ink `encre01`.
2. Lower the bridge: carry the handle `poignee04` onto the pulley `pouli03` (clicking
   the other pulley `pouli02` also works).
3. Click the shirt `chemise`: **zone 18**, place the ink ([a03.md](../docs/a03.md)).
4. Walk off the far edge, past the bridge, to the hospital courtyard.

## [Hospital courtyard](../docs/hopiext.md) (scene 2) and [ward](../docs/hopiint.md) (scene 8)

1. Ring the bell (`cloche`, the ropes) or push a gate leaf: the gate opens.
2. Go in (`porteent`). In the ward take the candle `BOUGIE`, the lamp `LAMPE`, the cap
   `BONNET`, the cross `CROIX` (open the wardrobe `ARMOIRE` if needed).
3. Click `FOU`/`ECHIK`: **zone 13**, place the lamp and the cap ([a03.md](../docs/a03.md)).
4. Click Van Gogh's mirror `MIRROIRVG`: **zone 15**, place the mirror piece and the teapot
   ([a13.md](../docs/a13.md)).
5. Go out by the plaque `PLAK` (close to it). In the courtyard click the tree `arbre11`
   (within reach): **zone 14**, place the candle and the cross ([a13.md](../docs/a13.md)).

## Back to the [Night Café](../docs/cafe.md)

1. Museum (Backspace), café painting.
2. Click the lamp `lampe4`: **zone 4**, place the cards, the lighter and the apple
   ([a03.md](../docs/a03.md)).
3. Click the glove `gant`: **zone 5**, place the parasol ([a13.md](../docs/a13.md)).
4. Museum: the Auvers door is open. Continue with [Act 3](act3.md).
