## Museum: the first zone

1. Walk up to the robot: it speaks.
2. When it has finished, a star appears: click `etoile`, drop it into the bar.
3. Click the `vase`: **zone 0**, place the star, take the sunflower to the vase
   ([a14.md](../docs/a14.md)).
4. Click the cottage painting (`m01_02`).

## [Cottage](../docs/maisonet.md) (scene 3)

1. Click the spade `pelle`: it digs and uncovers `terre`. Take the soil (`terre`).
2. Take the feather (`plume`).
3. Click the nest `nid`: **zone 1**, place the soil ([a01.md](../docs/a01.md)).
4. Go through the door `porte04` (close to it) to the potato eaters.

## [Potato eaters](../docs/mangeurs.md) (scene 4)

1. Open the stove door (`portepoel`).
2. Carry the faggot (`fagot`) onto the `fire`: it burns.
3. Carry the log (`buche`) onto the `fire`: it burns and the kettle heats.
4. Wait until the kettle steams, then take it (`theieres`).
5. Take the potato (`patat01`).
6. The baby cries: click the window (`fenetre`) to shut it (it also shuts by itself).
7. Click the cradle `bersso`: **zone 2**, place the kettle and the feather
   ([a01.md](../docs/a01.md)).
8. Click the pot `POT`: **zone 3**, place the potato ([a11.md](../docs/a11.md)).
9. Back to the museum (Backspace): the Arles door is open. Continue with [Act 2](act2.md).
