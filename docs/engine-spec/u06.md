# U06: the orangery garden and the clown

Everything U06's unit code (`U06.cpp`, vtable `0x00439760`, E-0390) does on top of the
generic scene: the start, the clown who shoots when the player comes near, the three click
handlers, and the way down into U07. Conventions are those of `u01.md` and `u02.md`:
`RunFor`, `MoveTo` (100.0 = keep; no position = keep), `LookAt`, "run node to f",
`Say(character, line)`, "effect X at P" (effects emitter, group 3), **suspend** /
**resume**, `Mnn` = the INFOACT record with id nn (`infoact.py --file Data/U06/INFOACT.BIN`),
"show X" / "hide X (no collision)" (E-0272), "fade to black over ms" (U01 "caught" step 7).

`s` = 12 (`SCENE.BIN`: ambient 90/90/90, scale 12, FOV 90, sphere radius 20, Z offset 40;
E-0393), so eye height h = 18. U06 has no load hook of its own (vtable `+0x10` is the
generic load) and no save chunk (`+8`/`+0xc` are the generic `Scene_RestoreState` /
`Scene_WriteState`).

## Every frame (`U06_UpdateFrameLogic`, E-0391)

After the generic render:

1. **Safe zone**: the eye (x, y) is safe when y > 280, or when −800 < x and y < 230
   (strictly; the other bounds are ±100000).
2. If safe: **stop**. Else d = distance from the eye to the clown hotspot's position:
   - not `shooting`: if d ≤ 330, **start**;
   - `shooting`: if d > 330, **stop**.
3. If `shooting` and the clown's node is paused (its clip ended): set frame 1, running;
   effect `Tir` at the eye; `shots` += 1; when `shots` > 2, **shot down**.

**Start**: the clown's node running; `shooting` := true. **Stop**: only if `shooting`: run
the clown's node to frame 49 (it pauses there); `shooting` := false, `shots` := 0.

So a clip (49 frames at 20 fps, 2.45 s) ends before each shot, and the third shot, about
7.4 s after coming in range, is fatal. Walking out of range or into the safe zone resets
the count. Coming back after a stop at 49 fires almost at once (one frame to 50).

**Shot down** (`U06_ShotDown`): suspend; `RunFor(800)`; `MoveTo(800, keep, keep yaw,
pitch 2.0)`; `MoveTo(1200, keep, yaw + 0.5, pitch 2.76)`; `MoveTo(1400, keep, yaw + π)`
(each reads the yaw again); `shots` := 0; fade to black over 600 ms; `RunFor(1000)`; game
over (game vtable `+0x14`: stop every sound, open `OptionLoad`, E-0082).

## Input hook (`U06_HandleInput`, E-0391)

The generic input first (`movement.md`). Then, while Up or Down is held: (yaw, pitch) =
polar angles of normalise(eye − clown hotspot position) (`X3d_Convert_To_Polar`), and the
clown turns to that yaw.

### The clown turns (`Hotspot_TurnToYaw`, E-0391)

A hotspot keeps a yaw (initially 0; U06 sets 3π/2), a pitch and a matrix M (the object's
local matrix when the hotspot was created at INFOOBJ time). Turning to yaw y:
M := M · Rz(yaw_old − y) (`X3d_Make_Rotation_Matrice_Z`, `X3d_Matrice_Mult(out, M, R)`);
yaw := y; pitch stored (unused); the hotspot's "turned" flag := 1. From then on, every
animation tick, after the nodes have posed their objects, each turned hotspot's object gets
local := local · M (`Hotspots_ApplyStoredMatrices`). The clown's node poses its root every
tick, so the product does not accumulate. Whether M's load-time part shows up twice is
Q-0170.

The clown only turns while the player walks forward or back; turning on the spot or
standing still leaves him as he was.

### Waking the man (`UseArrosoir`)

1. Suspend. Show `*U06_17` (the can reappears). Its node: frame 1, running.
2. Clip `reveil` (`Anim/U06_18/reveil.A3D`, 1..40) in slot 1 of `*U06_18`'s node, not
   active, loop off.
3. half = (can's last − first) · 0.5 = 74.5. While the can's node runs and Enter is not
   held: G = the can object's global position; cut the eye to (G.x − 3.09, G.y − 8.542,
   eye z); `LookAt(0, (−1058.87, 406.222, 15.2054))` (a cut plus one frame); the first
   time the can's frame > half − 15: effect `s4_13` at the eye; while its frame > half:
   make the `reveil` slot active (it plays).
4. `RunFor(0)` while the `reveil` clip runs and Enter is not held.
5. `Say(U06_18, d4_12)` (26.2 s). `MoveTo(4000, (−1049.73, 395.246, 25.41), yaw −2.8,
   pitch 0.77)`; `RunFor(0)` while the talker talks.
6. `MoveTo(1000, same, yaw −5.2, pitch 1.33)`. Hide `*U06_18` (no collision). Resume.

### Down the drain (`UseBiche`)

1. Suspend. Cover node `*U06_21`: loop off, running; effect `s4_15` at the eye;
   `RunFor(0)` until it pauses.
2. `MoveTo(1000, (−685.55, −1224.05, 25))`; `MoveTo(1000, (−685.295, −1224.03, −7.15),
   yaw 4.28)`; `RunFor(1000)`; `MoveTo(800, keep, yaw 4.11, pitch 2.777)`.
3. Cover node backward, running; effect `s4_15`; `RunFor(0)` until it pauses (the cover
   closes over the player).
4. Go to unit 7 (game `+4(7)`: scene `U07.x3d`). No video, no resume.
